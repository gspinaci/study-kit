# A Tiny Book on Linear Models

*Written as a self-contained source for the study-kit example. Licensed CC BY 4.0.*

## Preface

This booklet covers the smallest useful core of linear regression: fitting a linear model by least squares, and stabilizing that fit with regularization.

**Background.** Readers should be comfortable with matrix products, transposes and inverses, and with taking derivatives of simple functions of several variables.

**How to read this book.** Chapter 1 is required for everything that follows. Chapter 2 builds directly on the normal equations of Chapter 1; readers interested only in ordinary least squares may stop after Chapter 1.

**Exercises.** Each chapter ends with exercises. They are part of the text: later chapters assume the results of earlier exercises.
